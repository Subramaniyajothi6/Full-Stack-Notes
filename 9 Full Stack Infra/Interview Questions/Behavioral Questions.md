# Behavioral Questions

## STAR framework
**S**ituation → **T**ask → **A**ction → **R**esult.

## Top questions
- Tell me about yourself.
- Most challenging bug you've debugged.
- A time you disagreed with a teammate.
- A project you're proud of.
- Time you missed a deadline.
- Why us / why this role?
- Where do you see yourself in 3 years?

## Tips
- Concrete metrics in the result
- Show ownership and learning
- Avoid blaming teammates
- Tailor stories to the role's competencies
