# What is Express

> Minimal, unopinionated Node web framework. Adds routing, middleware, and helpers on top of [[HTTP Module|http]].

## Why it matters
- De-facto standard for Node REST APIs
- Tiny core, plug in what you need
- Mature ecosystem (auth, validation, security)

## Alternatives
- **Fastify** — faster, schema-driven
- **Koa** — modern async middleware
- **NestJS** — Angular-style framework on top of Express/Fastify
